English

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Biblia: Swahili & English (the “App”), developed by LeonTechForge (“we”, “us”), handles information. We built the App to let you read the Bible, not to collect data about you.

1. Information we do not collect

The App has no user accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or files, and we do not sell any information.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone so the App can work, and is never sent to us:

  • Bookmarks, highlight colours and personal notes on verses
  • Reading plan progress and reading streak
  • Your last reading position
  • Settings such as language, light/dark theme, font size, English translation, sound and daily reminder time

This information stays on your device. It is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, native, interstitial and app-open ads, plus optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a period without ads — scripture is never locked behind an ad). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version and how you interact with ads. This is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google’s own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice from the App’s settings. On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Permissions

  • Internet: used to load ads.
  • Notifications: used only if you turn on the daily reading reminder. You can turn it off at any time in the App or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup: lets your daily reminder keep working after the phone restarts.

5. Read aloud and sharing

The “listen” feature uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine. When you choose to share or copy a verse, the text goes only to the app or place you select.

6. Children

The App is suitable for general audiences but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data security and deletion

Because your personal data stays on your device, you are in control of it. See our Data Deletion page for how to remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer, LeonTechForge, using the developer contact details shown on the App’s Google Play Store page.

Kiswahili

Sera hii ya Faragha inaeleza jinsi programu ya Android Biblia: Swahili & English (“Programu”), iliyotengenezwa na LeonTechForge (“sisi”), inavyoshughulikia taarifa. Tumeitengeneza Programu hii ili kukuwezesha kusoma Biblia, si kukusanya taarifa zako.

1. Taarifa ambazo hatukusanyi

Programu haina akaunti za watumiaji, haihitaji kujisajili na haina seva zetu. Hatukusanyi jina lako, barua pepe, namba ya simu, anwani za watu, mahali ulipo, picha wala mafaili, na hatuuzi taarifa yoyote.

2. Taarifa zinazohifadhiwa kwenye simu yako tu

Taarifa zifuatazo huhifadhiwa ndani ya simu yako ili Programu ifanye kazi, na hazitumwi kwetu kamwe:

  • Alama za mistari (vipendwa), rangi za alama na maelezo yako binafsi
  • Maendeleo ya mpango wa kusoma na mfululizo wa siku za kusoma
  • Mahali ulipoishia kusoma
  • Mipangilio kama lugha, hali ya mwanga/giza, ukubwa wa herufi, tafsiri ya Kiingereza, sauti na muda wa kikumbusho

Taarifa hizi hubaki kwenye simu yako. Zinafutwa unapofuta data ya Programu au unapoiondoa Programu.

3. Matangazo (Google AdMob)

Programu ni ya bure na inaonyesha matangazo kutoka Google AdMob (matangazo ya bango, ya ndani ya orodha, ya skrini nzima na ya kufungua programu, pamoja na matangazo ya zawadi unayoweza kuchagua kutazama ili upate muda bila matangazo — maandiko hayafungwi kamwe nyuma ya tangazo). Ili kuonyesha na kupima matangazo, Google inaweza kukusanya taarifa kama kitambulisho cha matangazo cha kifaa chako, anwani ya IP, aina ya simu, toleo la mfumo wa uendeshaji na jinsi unavyoingiliana na matangazo. Taarifa hizi hukusanywa na Google, si sisi, na zinashughulikiwa chini ya sera za Google:

  • Sera ya Faragha ya Google
  • Jinsi Google inavyotumia taarifa kutoka kwa programu zinazotumia huduma zake

Pale sheria inapohitaji (kwa mfano Ulaya na Uingereza), Programu huomba ridhaa yako kabla ya kuonyesha matangazo yaliyobinafsishwa. Unaweza kubadilisha uamuzi huo kwenye mipangilio ya Programu. Kwenye simu yoyote ya Android unaweza pia kubadilisha au kufuta kitambulisho chako cha matangazo, au kukataa matangazo yaliyobinafsishwa, kupitia Settings → Google → Ads (au Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Ruhusa

  • Intaneti: hutumika kupakia matangazo.
  • Arifa: hutumika tu ukiwasha kikumbusho cha kusoma kila siku. Unaweza kukizima wakati wowote ndani ya Programu au kwenye mipangilio ya Android.
  • Kuanza simu inapowashwa: huwezesha kikumbusho chako kuendelea kufanya kazi baada ya simu kuzimwa na kuwashwa upya.

5. Kusikiliza na kushiriki

Kipengele cha “sikiliza” kinatumia injini ya sauti (text-to-speech) iliyo ndani ya simu yako. Unapochagua kushiriki au kunakili mstari, maandishi huenda tu kwenye programu au mahali utakapochagua.

6. Watoto

Programu inafaa kwa watu wa rika zote lakini haijalengwa mahususi kwa watoto walio chini ya miaka 13. Hatukusanyi kwa kujua taarifa binafsi za watoto.

7. Usalama na kufuta data

Kwa kuwa taarifa zako binafsi zinabaki kwenye simu yako, wewe ndiye mwenye udhibiti. Tazama ukurasa wa Kufuta Data kujua jinsi ya kuzifuta.

8. Mabadiliko ya sera hii

Tunaweza kusasisha sera hii mara kwa mara. Mabadiliko yatawekwa kwenye ukurasa huu pamoja na tarehe mpya ya kuanza kutumika.

9. Mawasiliano

Maswali kuhusu sera hii yanaweza kutumwa kwa msanidi programu, LeonTechForge, kupitia mawasiliano ya msanidi yaliyoonyeshwa kwenye ukurasa wa Programu katika Google Play Store.
