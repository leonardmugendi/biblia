Biblia: Swahili & English has no accounts and no servers of its own. LeonTechForge does not hold any of your personal data, so there is nothing stored with us to request deletion of. Everything the App saves stays on your own device, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

What the App stores on your device

  • Bookmarks, highlight colours and notes
  • Reading plan progress and streak
  • Last reading position and app settings

Option 1: Clear the App’s data (keeps the App installed)

  1. Open your phone’s Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and select Biblia: Swahili & English.
  3. Tap Storage (or Storage & cache).
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm.

Option 2: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling the App from your phone or from the Google Play Store permanently deletes all of its data from your device.

To delete individual items

Inside the App, long-press any verse and tap Remove bookmark, or clear a note’s text and save it.

Advertising data (Google)

Ads in the App are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID. To reset or delete it, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) on your phone. To manage or delete data held by Google, visit Google Data & Privacy. See also our Privacy Policy.

Questions

You can reach the developer, LeonTechForge, using the developer contact details shown on the App’s Google Play Store page.
